# Community Code of Conduct

Velora welcomes contributors of different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, technical and constructive.

- Discuss the code or behavior, not the person.
- Give review feedback with enough context to act on it.
- Assume contributors may be new to GNOME Shell, GJS or open-source collaboration.
- Credit prior work and preserve required attribution.
- Keep disagreements focused on evidence, reproducibility and project constraints.
- Do not pressure contributors to merge unfinished or unsafe changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

The project does not accept:

- harassment, threats or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks or sustained hostility;
- publishing private information;
- deliberate disruption, spam or deceptive contributions;
- knowingly removing license/provenance notices that must remain.

## Technical disagreements

Velora has a strong architecture contract: native GNOME behavior and ownership come first.

When contributors disagree about an implementation:

1. state the user-visible goal;
2. provide reproducible evidence where possible;
3. compare lifecycle, performance and native-Shell impact;
4. prefer the smallest change that preserves the project contract.

Maintainers may decline a change that conflicts with the product architecture even when the implementation itself works.

## Reporting problems

For ordinary project behavior, use Issues or Discussions.

For abusive behavior that is appropriate to report through GitHub, use GitHub's reporting and abuse tools. Do not post sensitive personal information publicly in an issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
